# Compensation Structure Guides

Plain-language guides to the compensation structures MLMForge supports. No configuration syntax, no code. Just how things work and what options you have.

Read them in order. Each guide builds on concepts from the ones before it.

| # | Guide | What it covers |
|---|-------|---------------|
| 1 | [Fundamentals](fundamentals.md) | Volume, eligibility, and caps. Concepts that apply to every structure. Read this first. |
| 2 | [Unilevel](unilevel.md) | The simplest structure. Unlimited width, level-based commissions. |
| 3 | [Binary](binary.md) | Two legs per distributor. Commissions based on matching volume between legs. |
| 4 | [Matrix](matrix.md) | Fixed width and depth. A constrained version of unilevel with spillover placement. |
| 5 | [Stairstep Breakaway](stairstep.md) | Groups break away at rank thresholds. The most complex structure. |
| 6 | [Generation](generation.md) | Commissions based on leadership depth, not tree depth. The generation walk and its configuration options. |
